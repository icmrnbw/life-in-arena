Survive 20 waves with 18 heroes. Shop, level up, beat the bosses. Offline.
Emberpit is a hero arena survival game made for phones.

Pick one of 18 heroes, each with their own way to fight: build walls of fire, command a brood, lay traps, parry blades, freeze the pack or stop time. Then survive 20 waves of monsters in the Pit, with a boss every fifth wave and the Warden waiting at the end.

• 18 heroes, 4 skills each, and skins to unlock
• Every wave has a twist: bloodthirsty foes, magic wards, phantoms, exploding elites
• Shop between waves: 28 items and recipes, plus training for permanent stats
• Armor and magic resist matter: adapt your build to what is coming
• Smash into barrel stacks to fight in narrow corridors
• Delve: a dark, branching dungeon with traps, shrines, vaults and relics
• Souls, the Altar, Heat levels, hero Mastery and daily Bounties keep every run worth it
• AI allies, rival duels, Endless, Daily Run and Practice modes
• Weather that changes the fight: rain, fog, gale, emberfall, blood moon
• Plays fully offline. No ads, no purchases, no account.
