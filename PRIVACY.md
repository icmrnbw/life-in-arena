# Emberpit privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Emberpit is a single-player game. It works fully offline.

## What we collect

Nothing. Emberpit does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no network code.

## What stays on your phone

The game saves your settings, records, Souls, hero Mastery and a run in progress on your device. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may back these files up to your own Google account, as it does for other apps. We never see them.

If the game closes unexpectedly, it keeps a short crash note on your device. It is only shared if you tap "Copy report" and paste it somewhere yourself.

## Links that leave the game

The Feedback and Privacy policy buttons open a web page in your browser. Anything you write there is sent to that website, under that website's own privacy policy, not through the game.

## Permissions

Vibration, to buzz on big hits. You can turn it off in Settings.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at https://github.com/icmrnbw/life-in-arena/issues.
